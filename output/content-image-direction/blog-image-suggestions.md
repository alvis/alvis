# Blog image suggestions

These concepts are grounded in the actual argument of each essay rather than its title alone. The recommended concept is the strongest lead image; the alternatives can become inline figures, social crops, or later variants.

## Configuration Should Have a Memory: Presetter v9

**Recommended — Configuration strata**

A cutaway block of layered paper configuration: older decisions compacted into stable indigo strata, a visible fault where v9 changes the namespace and baseline, and a fresh top layer being pinned into place. A thin rust provenance line runs through every layer. Use the visual language of **16 — Memory Stratigraphy**.

- Composition: landscape cross-section with the important fault safely inside the centre crop.
- Meaning: configuration is organizational memory, not copied files.
- Alt direction: “Layered paper strata preserve engineering decisions across a visible version change.”

**Alternative — The inspectable release workshop**

An exploded assembly of presets, runtime foundations, release provenance, and migration pieces on an open workbench. Nothing is hidden inside a black box. Use **19 — The Open Workshop**.

**Alternative — The migration bridge**

Two coherent paper ecosystems on either side of a narrow bridge: old package names enter, scoped packages leave, while the local project remains recognizably itself.

## The Context Layer Is the Product

**Recommended — The decision surface**

Many translucent context sheets—decisions, constraints, people, promises, unresolved assumptions—converge beneath one clear working surface. Each source remains traceable instead of dissolving into a generic glow. Use **11 — Archive of Intent** with the traceability of **17 — The Provenance Loom**.

- Composition: central decision plane with layered evidence entering from the edges.
- Meaning: trust accumulates in the context layer, not in the isolated model response.
- Alt direction: “Traceable layers of working context converge beneath a clear decision surface.”

**Alternative — Context as quiet infrastructure**

A building section where memory, provenance, and audit trails live in the walls and foundations while purposeful work happens calmly above.

**Alternative — The continuity table**

One long worktable crosses several moments in time; decisions and drafts remain in place as people and tasks change around it.

## Why Agent Demos Break in Production

**Recommended — The missing backstage**

A pristine demonstration stage at the front, with the cutaway behind it revealing the real production apparatus: memory boundaries, permissions, recovery routes, feedback, and a human review point. The stage mechanism fails wherever one of those systems is absent. Use **15 — Bounded Delegation**.

- Composition: split depth rather than a literal before/after divide.
- Meaning: the impressive action is easy; responsibility around the action is the product.
- Alt direction: “A polished agent demonstration is supported by visible memory, permission, recovery, and review systems backstage.”

**Alternative — The action with four shadows**

One simple outgoing action casts four structural shadows labelled only through form: memory, tools, guardrails, and feedback.

**Alternative — Recovery is part of the route**

A route branches before a risky threshold, with inspection, reversal, escalation, and safe stopping shown as first-class paths rather than error states.

## Designing Tools That Disappear Into the Work

**Recommended — The quiet studio**

An inhabited studio section where light, drawers, tools, and memory support the maker without demanding attention. The only visually dominant element is the work in progress. Use **13 — The Quiet Studio**.

- Composition: wide architectural section with the maker and work centred; interface-like details remain peripheral.
- Meaning: good tools make the work more visible than the system around it.
- Alt direction: “A calm studio supports a maker through well-placed tools, memory, light, and unobtrusive structure.”

**Alternative — The honest threshold**

A tool appears only at the moment judgment is required, like a small handrail at a difficult step, then disappears from the path.

**Alternative — The instrument at rest**

A pencil, command surface, notebook margin, and provenance trace share one visual grammar; none is presented as the hero object.

## On the Context Harness

**Recommended — The model has a place to stand**

A small neutral model-core stands on a carefully built platform composed of tools, memory, retrieval, policy, and curated evidence. The platform is visually richer and more consequential than the core. Use **19 — The Open Workshop** with the atmosphere of **14 — Probability Observatory**.

- Composition: the platform occupies most of the frame; the model-core is intentionally modest.
- Meaning: the surrounding judgment, not the commodity model, makes situated work possible.
- Alt direction: “A modest model core stands on an inspectable platform of memory, tools, retrieval, policy, and evidence.”

**Alternative — The borrowed notebook**

Two notebooks support the same extended-cognition mechanism: one visibly belongs to the user, the other is curated by an unseen third party.

**Alternative — Furniture in the context room**

A large empty room becomes useful only when evidence, constraints, memory, and tools are deliberately arranged within it.

## A Harness in Eighty Lines

**Recommended — The whole loop on one bench**

A compact workbench holds four connected mechanisms: call, route, remember, stop. The complete rust-red loop fits on one surface and can be followed without hidden machinery. Use **19 — The Open Workshop**.

- Composition: landscape, four stations, one continuous readable route, generous crop safety.
- Meaning: smallness creates legibility and makes encoded judgment arguable.
- Alt direction: “Four inspectable mechanisms on one workbench form a complete model, tool, memory, and stopping loop.”

**Alternative — The single-sheet harness**

One folded technical sheet contains the entire apparatus; unfolding it reveals the policy gate and stopping mechanism that frameworks usually obscure.

**Alternative — The stopping weight**

A looping mechanism would continue forever, but a carefully calibrated physical weight stops it when repetition, cost, or drift becomes excessive.

## The Polite Agent

**Recommended — The calibrated pause**

A prepared action stops at a graceful threshold. A human hand can approve it, while a smaller safe alternative remains visible. The pause reads as care rather than obstruction. Use **20 — The Calibrated Pause**.

- Composition: large negative space around the held action; avoid literal chat bubbles or robots.
- Meaning: usefulness includes restraint, refusal, silence, and making the next human move easy.
- Alt direction: “A prepared action pauses at a considered threshold while approval and a safer alternative remain available.”

**Alternative — Two instruments in tune**

A human and agent are represented as two instruments sharing a score; rests and held notes matter as much as played notes.

**Alternative — The note on the locked door**

A closed threshold includes a smaller open path beside it, visually expressing refusal with a useful alternative rather than refusal by reflex.

## Shared production constraints

- Keep feature images at the existing landscape ratio, with the central idea surviving a roughly 600 × 338 thumbnail crop.
- Avoid readable interface text, faux code, generic robots, glowing brains, chat bubbles, and stock “AI network” imagery.
- Preserve the site palette: warm paper, deep indigo, pale blue, restrained rust, muted sage and dusty lilac.
- Use one dominant metaphor per image. Supporting diagrams should clarify the argument rather than decorate it.
- Keep provenance lines and human marks thin enough to feel deliberate but strong enough to survive thumbnail reduction.
